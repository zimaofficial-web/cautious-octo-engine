# Creative & Visual Design Guidelines

> [!NOTE]
> **Context Isolation:** This document governs non-software creative and visual tasks. No programming syntax, code formatting, or developer constraints apply here.

---

## 1. Graphic Design & Visual Storytelling Principles

### 1.1 Intentional Composition & Balance
- **Focal Point:** Every graphic, poster, or slide must have a single dominant visual element that captures the eye within 1 second.
- **Negative Space (Breathing Room):** White space is a functional design element, not empty canvas to be filled. Give typography and imagery space to breathe.
- **Visual Flow:** Guide the viewer’s eye along a natural path (Z-pattern for quick promotional scans, F-pattern for informational posters).

### 1.2 Color Harmony & Mood
- Build palettes using established psychological associations:
  - *Monochromatic:* Elegant, focused, unified.
  - *Analogous:* Calm, natural, cohesive.
  - *Complementary:* Dynamic, high-energy, high-contrast callouts.
- Limit palettes to 3–4 core colors per composition to avoid visual noise.

## 2. Branding & Logo Exploration

### 2.1 Logo Exploration Framework
When exploring identity designs, evaluate each concept against the **L.I.M.S.** criteria:
- **Legible:** Can the mark and logotype be read clearly at 16x16px (favicon) and on a billboard?
- **Impactful:** Does it evoke an immediate emotional reaction aligned with the brand essence?
- **Memorable:** Is the silhouette distinct enough to be drawn from memory on a napkin?
- **Scalable / Versatile:** Does it function in monochrome (pure black on white) as well as full color?

### 2.2 Brand Identity Artifacts
- **Color Systems:** Primary, secondary, neutral, and accent values with CMYK, RGB, and Pantone references.
- **Typography Suite:** Primary brand font, secondary editorial font, and accent display typeface.
- **Brand Voice Keywords:** Define 3–5 guiding brand adjectives (e.g., "Understated, Authoritative, Kinetic").

## 3. Marketing Collateral: Posters, Flyers & Social Graphics

### 3.1 Posters & Physical Flyers
- **3-Second Distance Rule:** The headline and hero visual must be understandable from 10 feet away.
- **Information Hierarchy:**
  1. *The Hook:* Bold headline or visual statement (Top 40%).
  2. *The Details:* Date, venue, core proposition (Middle 35%).
  3. *The Action:* Clear call-to-action, URL, QR code, or contact details (Bottom 25%).

### 3.2 Social Media Graphics (LinkedIn, X, Instagram)
- **Aspect Ratio Awareness:**
  - Square (`1:1`) & Vertical (`4:5` / `9:16`): Prioritize for mobile feed dominance.
  - Landscape (`16:9` / `1.91:1`): For link previews and header banners.
- **Thumb-Stopping Contrast:** High contrast between text and background imagery. Avoid placing thin text over busy photographic backgrounds without an overlay.

## 4. Presentation & Slide Deck Design

- **The "One Idea Per Slide" Rule:** Never crowd multiple distinct thoughts onto a single slide. Split them into sequential slides.
- **Death to Bulleted Lists:** Replace long walls of bullet points with:
  - High-impact visual callouts (large single numbers, e.g., "78%").
  - Clean two- or three-column card comparisons.
  - Full-bleed photography with a single bold sentence.
- **Consistent Grid:** Maintain identical margins, logo placements, and slide title positioning across all slides to avoid visual jumping during transitions.

## 5. AI Image Generation Prompting Architecture

When generating creative image prompts (for Midjourney, Imagen, DALL-E, or Stable Diffusion), structure prompts using the **Subject + Environment + Style + Lighting + Camera** formula:

```text
[Core Subject / Action], [Environment / Background Setting], [Art Style / Medium], [Lighting / Atmosphere], [Composition / Lens Specifications]
```

### Prompt Construction Principles:
- **Avoid Vague Buzzwords:** Do not use "photorealistic", "ultra realistic", or "4K". Instead, specify concrete camera and lighting physics:
  - *Camera:* "Shot on 35mm film", "50mm f/1.4 lens", "shallow depth of field", "cinematic wide angle".
  - *Lighting:* "Golden hour rim light", "dramatic chiaroscuro contrast", "soft diffused window illumination", "overcast moody daylight".
  - *Texture / Medium:* "Tactile matte paper texture", "fine art editorial photography", "minimalist gouache illustration with textured brushstrokes".
